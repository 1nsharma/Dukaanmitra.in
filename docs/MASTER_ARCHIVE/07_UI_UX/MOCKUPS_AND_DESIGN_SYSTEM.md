# 07 UI/UX: Design Tokens, Visual Language & 6 Core Mockup Modules

## 1. Design Tokens & Visual Principles
- **Target Audience**: Indian Kirana shopkeepers and Tier-2/3 retail owners.
- **Visual Style**: High contrast, bold typography, tactile buttons, intuitive color codes.
  - Emerald Green (`#10B981`): In-stock, paid invoices, healthy margins.
  - Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`): Low-stock warning, pending approval tickets.
  - Rose Red (`#EF4444`): Out of stock, overdue Udhaar (>30 days), blocked sensitive operations.
  - Slate Dark (`#0F172A`): Clean professional high-contrast background.
- **Interaction Model**: Minimum typing required; voice-first buttons, quick barcode scanning, 1-tap quick actions.

---

## 2. The 6 Core Mockup Modules

### A. WhatsAppMockup
- Interactive live simulation of a WhatsApp conversation between customer/dukaandar and Munim AI.
- Features: Voice note waveform playback, interactive product card buttons, 1-tap UPI payment chips.

### B. InventoryMockup
- Visual shelf and product list with thumbnail photos, SKU badges, batch numbers, and expiry alerts.
- Filter chips: "All Stock", "Low Stock (<5)", "Fast Moving", "Dead Stock (>45 days)".

### C. BillingMockup
- Fast counter POS interface: Barcode scan input, automatic tax computation, split-tender (Cash + UPI), and 1-click WhatsApp receipt dispatch.

### D. UdhaarMockup
- Digital Khata ledger card: Customer name, phone, outstanding amount, days overdue, and instant "Send Reminder on WhatsApp" button.

### E. AnalyticsMockup
- Real-time business metrics: Daily GMV, gross margin %, top selling SKUs, and hourly footfall graphs.

### F. CRMMockup
- Customer directory with lifetime value, average order value (AOV), last purchase date, and personalized WhatsApp offer composer.
